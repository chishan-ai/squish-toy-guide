# Needoh Dream Drop Review

The Needoh Dream Drop lives up to its name. Squeezing it feels like holding a little cloud. This teardrop-shaped fidget toy is one of the softest in the Needoh lineup.

## Cloud-Like Softness

The Dream Drop is incredibly soft, scoring a perfect 5/5 on squishiness. Its teardrop shape makes it easy to grip and squeeze with one hand, and the slow-rise return is hypnotically satisfying.

## Perfect for Relaxation

With virtually no noise and a calming, meditative squeeze feel, the Dream Drop is best suited for stress relief and quiet moments. It's a great choice for bedtime fidgeting.
