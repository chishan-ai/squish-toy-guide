# Needoh Snowball Review

Sometimes simplicity wins. The Needoh Snowball strips away novelty shapes and delivers pure, satisfying squeeze in a classic round form. Its frosty white finish gives it a winter-themed charm that works year-round.

## Design & Shape

The Snowball is a perfectly round sphere with a matte white finish that resembles a real snowball. No frills, no sculpted details. Just a clean, smooth ball that fits perfectly in your palm.

## Sensory Experience

At 4/5 squishiness, the Snowball offers a balanced squeeze, softer than the Football but firmer than the Gumdrop. The round shape means you can squeeze it evenly from any direction, making it one of the most versatile tactile experiences in the lineup.

## Durability

Scoring 4/5 on durability, the Snowball benefits from its simple shape. No protruding features or thin spots means fewer weak points. It bounces back consistently and holds its shape well over time.

## Who Is It For?

The Needoh Snowball is perfect for:
- **Sensory seekers** who want a straightforward squeeze experience
- **Therapists** looking for a clean, simple fidget tool
- **Kids 3+** for catch-and-squeeze play
- **Budget buyers**: it's one of the most affordable Needoh toys

## Where to Buy

Available at Amazon, Target, and dollar stores. Prices typically range from $6 to $9.

## Our Verdict

The Snowball proves that simple can be great. It's durable, affordable, and delivers a solid squeeze. A strong choice for anyone who wants a no-nonsense fidget ball.
