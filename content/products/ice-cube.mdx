# Needoh Ice Cube Review

The Needoh Ice Cube is the firmer cousin of the iconic Nice Cube. With a cooler, more resistant feel and exceptional durability, it's the choice for fidgeters who prefer a bit more resistance in their squeeze.

## How It Differs from the Nice Cube

Where the Nice Cube is ultra-squishy, the Ice Cube offers more pushback. It scores 3/5 on squishiness, satisfying but with a firmness that some users actually prefer. The tradeoff? Outstanding durability at 5/5.

## Built to Last

The Ice Cube is the most durable Needoh we've tested. The firmer material resists tearing and maintains its shape even after heavy, daily use.
