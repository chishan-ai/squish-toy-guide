# Needoh Jellyfish Review

The Needoh Jellyfish brings something unique to the squish toy world — stretchy tentacles combined with a squishy body. It's a multisensory experience that goes beyond simple squeezing.

## What Sets It Apart

While most Needoh toys focus on pure squishiness, the Jellyfish adds a stretching element. You can squeeze the body, pull the tentacles, or do both at once. It's like getting two fidget toys in one.

## Sensory Profile

The body is satisfyingly squishy (4/5) while the tentacles add a stretchy, tactile dimension. The noise level is low, and the overall size makes it great for desk play or on-the-go fidgeting.
