# Needoh Advent Calendar Review

The Needoh Advent Calendar has become one of the most sought-after holiday items in the fidget toy world, generating over 33,000 monthly searches as the season approaches. Behind each of the 24 doors lies a mini Needoh surprise.

## What You Get

The calendar contains 24 mini Needoh toys, each behind a numbered door. The minis are smaller versions of popular Needoh shapes: cubes, drops, balls, and other surprise forms. Each mini is roughly half the size of a standard Needoh toy.

## Sensory Experience

The minis score a solid 4/5 on squishiness. While slightly firmer than their full-size counterparts due to the smaller volume of fill, they still deliver that signature Needoh slow-rise squeeze. The variety means you get to experience different shapes and feels throughout the month.

## Durability & Size

At 3/5 durability, the minis are decent but less robust than full-size Needoh toys. The smaller size (2/5) makes them easy to lose but also great for little hands. We recommend keeping them in the calendar box or a small container.

## Who Is It For?

The Needoh Advent Calendar is perfect for:
- **Parents** looking for a non-chocolate advent calendar alternative
- **Fidget toy collectors** who want mini versions
- **Gift-givers** who need a standout holiday present
- **Teachers** who want classroom countdown rewards

## Where to Buy

Available at Amazon, Target, and specialty toy retailers. Prices typically range from $25 to $35. Stock tends to sell out fast in November, so buy early.

## Our Verdict

The Advent Calendar is the best Needoh gift during the holiday season. At roughly $1.25 per mini toy, it's excellent value, and the daily surprise factor makes it genuinely exciting for kids.
