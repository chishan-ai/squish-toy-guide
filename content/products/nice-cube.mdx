# Needoh Nice Cube Review

The Needoh Nice Cube is arguably the most iconic product in the Needoh lineup, and for good reason. This satisfyingly squishy cube has captured the attention of fidget toy enthusiasts worldwide, racking up over 227,000 monthly searches.

## What Makes the Nice Cube Special

The Nice Cube stands out from other fidget toys with its unique combination of squishiness and form factor. Unlike round stress balls that can roll away, the cube shape stays put on your desk while still offering incredibly satisfying squeeze feedback.

## Sensory Experience

The Nice Cube scores a perfect 5/5 on our squishiness scale. It has that signature Needoh slow-rise return that makes you want to squeeze it again and again. The noise level is minimal, a quiet, satisfying squish that won't disturb your coworkers.

## Durability

We've been impressed with the Nice Cube's durability. The outer membrane is resilient and bounces back to its original shape consistently. After months of daily use, our test cubes showed minimal wear.

## Who Is It For?

The Nice Cube is perfect for:
- **Desk workers** looking for a discreet fidget option
- **Students** who need something to keep their hands busy during study sessions
- **Anyone** dealing with stress or anxiety who benefits from tactile stimulation

## Where to Buy

The Needoh Nice Cube is available at major retailers including Amazon, Target, and specialty toy stores. Prices typically range from $8 to $12.

## Our Verdict

The Nice Cube earns its spot as the #1 Needoh product. It's affordable, satisfying, and durable. Everything you want in a desk fidget toy.
