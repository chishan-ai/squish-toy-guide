# Needoh Ice Cream Review

The Needoh Ice Cream takes the beloved Needoh squeeze formula and wraps it in one of the most adorable shapes in the lineup. Shaped like a classic soft-serve cone, this fidget toy is as fun to look at as it is to squeeze.

## Design & Shape

The Ice Cream stands upright and features a swirled top that mimics a real soft-serve cone. It comes in a range of pastel colors that look good enough to eat (but please don't). The cone portion provides a natural grip point, making it easy for small hands to hold.

## Sensory Experience

Scoring a perfect 5/5 on squishiness, the Ice Cream offers that signature Needoh slow-rise return. The super-soft fill makes it one of the gentlest squeezes in the collection. Noise is virtually nonexistent at 1/5, so it's great for quiet environments.

## Durability

At 3/5, the Ice Cream is average in durability. The sculpted shape means more surface area and potential stress points compared to simpler shapes like the Nice Cube. With careful use, it holds up well, but aggressive squeezing can wear the seams over time.

## Who Is It For?

The Needoh Ice Cream is perfect for:
- **Gift-givers** looking for a cute, affordable present
- **Collectors** who love novelty-shaped fidget toys
- **Young kids (3+)** who enjoy imaginative play
- **Desk decorators** who want a fun conversation starter

## Where to Buy

Available at Amazon, Target, and specialty toy retailers. Prices typically range from $7 to $10.

## Our Verdict

The Ice Cream is the most giftable Needoh toy. It trades a bit of durability for maximum charm, making it an ideal stocking stuffer or birthday party favor.
