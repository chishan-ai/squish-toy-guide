# Needoh Gummy Bear Review

The Needoh Gummy Bear is one of the cutest entries in the Needoh lineup, combining a candy-inspired shape with a stretchy, gummy-like texture that feels genuinely different from other Needoh toys.

## Design & Shape

Shaped like a classic gummy bear candy, this toy stands about 3 inches tall with distinct arms, legs, and ears. It comes in translucent candy colors (red, green, yellow, and orange) that look remarkably like real gummy bears. The translucent material lets you see the fill inside.

## Sensory Experience

At 4/5 squishiness, the Gummy Bear offers a unique stretchy feel. Unlike most Needoh toys that emphasize squeeze-and-return, the Gummy Bear also stretches slightly, giving it a dual tactile experience. It's nearly silent at 1/5 noise, perfect for quiet fidgeting.

## Durability & Stain Risk

Durability is moderate at 3/5. The limbs can be stress points if pulled too aggressively. The stain risk is higher at 4/5. The translucent material tends to attract dust and can leave residue on light surfaces. We recommend washing hands after extended use.

## Who Is It For?

The Needoh Gummy Bear is perfect for:
- **Kids 3+** who love candy-themed toys
- **Sensory explorers** who enjoy stretch-and-squeeze combos
- **Collectors** building a complete Needoh collection
- **TikTok fans**: the translucent look is very camera-friendly

## Where to Buy

Available at Amazon, Target, and toy specialty stores. Prices typically range from $7 to $10.

## Our Verdict

The Gummy Bear stands out with its stretchy texture and adorable design. Just be mindful of the stain risk and handle the limbs gently. Treat it right, and it's one of the most fun Needoh toys to play with.
