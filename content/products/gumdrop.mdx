# Needoh Gumdrop Review

The Needoh Gumdrop is one of the softest toys in the entire Needoh collection. Shaped like a gumdrop candy, this little fidget delivers an incredibly satisfying squeeze that's hard to put down.

## The Feel

The Gumdrop is impossibly soft. It scores a perfect 5/5 on squishiness and is one of the quietest Needoh toys we've tested. The dome shape fits perfectly in your palm, making it ideal for one-handed squeezing.

## Best For

The Gumdrop is ideal for stress relief and younger kids (3+) thanks to its small size and ultra-soft feel. It's a great starter Needoh for anyone new to the brand.
