# What Is Needoh?

Needoh is a line of sensory fidget toys made by Schylling, known for their incredibly satisfying squishy feel. Each Needoh toy is filled with a non-toxic, dough-like material that provides a unique tactile experience.

## Why Are Needoh Toys So Popular?

Needoh toys have exploded in popularity thanks to TikTok and social media, where their satisfying squeeze has captivated millions. They're used for stress relief, sensory play, and desk fidgeting.
