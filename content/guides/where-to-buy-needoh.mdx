## How to Spot Fake Needoh Toys

Counterfeit Needoh toys are increasingly common. Here's how to identify authentic products:

1. **Check the packaging** — Real Needoh has the Schylling logo and consistent branding
2. **Feel the material** — Authentic Needoh has a distinct, non-greasy dough-like fill
3. **Buy from authorized retailers** — Stick to the stores listed above
4. **Price check** — If it's dramatically cheaper than retail, it's likely fake
