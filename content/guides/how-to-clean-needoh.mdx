# How to Clean Needoh Toys — Care & Maintenance Guide

Needoh toys get dirty — it's inevitable when you're squeezing them all day. Here's how to keep them clean, extend their lifespan, and deal with common issues.

## Basic Cleaning

The safest way to clean a Needoh toy:

1. **Dampen a soft cloth** with lukewarm water
2. **Add a tiny drop of mild dish soap** to the cloth
3. **Gently wipe** the surface of the Needoh toy
4. **Rinse the cloth** and wipe again to remove soap residue
5. **Pat dry** with a clean towel and let air dry completely

**Important**: Never submerge a Needoh toy in water. The material is not waterproof, and soaking can damage the fill.

## Removing Stickiness

Needoh toys can develop a sticky surface over time. Here's how to fix it:

- **Cornstarch method**: Lightly dust the surface with cornstarch, then wipe clean. This absorbs excess oils and restores a smooth feel.
- **Baby powder**: Works the same way as cornstarch. Apply lightly and wipe off.
- **Rubbing alcohol**: For stubborn stickiness, a quick wipe with isopropyl alcohol can help. Use sparingly.

## Removing Lint & Hair

The Needoh surface attracts lint and pet hair like a magnet. To remove:

- **Lint roller**: The easiest method — just roll over the surface
- **Tape**: Press packaging tape against the surface to lift debris
- **Damp cloth**: A slightly damp microfiber cloth picks up most particles

## Storage Tips

Proper storage extends your Needoh's life significantly:

- **Keep in the original container** or a sealed plastic bag
- **Store at room temperature** — avoid extreme heat or cold
- **Keep away from direct sunlight** — UV can degrade the material
- **Separate from other toys** — Needoh can pick up colors from other materials
- **Wash hands before use** — oils and dirt from hands are the #1 cause of buildup

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Needoh has a tear or hole
Unfortunately, there's no reliable way to repair a torn Needoh. The internal fill will begin to leak. Replace the toy.

### Needoh lost its squishiness
This usually means it's been exposed to temperature extremes. Try bringing it to room temperature and squeezing it gently for a few minutes to redistribute the fill.

### Needoh left a stain
Some Needoh colors can leave marks on light-colored surfaces. Use a mild cleaner or Magic Eraser on the affected surface. For clothing stains, treat with dish soap before washing.

## Our Advice

Clean your Needoh toys once a week if you use them daily. Store them in their containers when not in use. With basic care, a Needoh toy can last for months of regular squeezing.
